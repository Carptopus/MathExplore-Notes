## Result and boundary

For the arithmetic symmetric modulus

$$
S(A)=\frac{|A|+|A^*|}{2},
$$

the manuscript determines the exact sharp Schatten constant in dimension two for
every number of summands $m\ge2$ and every finite interior exponent
$1<p<\infty$, as an explicit one-variable maximum, with real rank-one extremizers.
It also proves the exact Frobenius constant $\sqrt{3\sqrt2}/2$ in arbitrary
ambient dimension when the sum has rank at most two.

Beyond those exact layers, it proves that the natural nilpotent candidate curve
fails in some finite dimension for every $1<p<2$, and proves the cubic candidate
bound on two natural rank-two Gram strata: equal nonzero singular values and
commuting compressed positive factor.

This is a partial exponent--dimension--rank phase diagram. It does not solve the
full MI-09 problem, arbitrary rank in dimension at least three, or the full
rank-two problem for finite $p>2$. The $p=\infty$ endpoint is prior work of Zhang
and is used only as background. The accessible preprints did not directly cover
the four retained theorem groups, but the Zhang and Bourin--Lee versions of record
were unavailable; accordingly no worldwide-priority claim is made.
