# Classification of central graphical zonotopal algebras

Version DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23058037](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23058037) · [All versions](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22542288)

[Manuscript PDF](central-graphical-zonotopal-classification.pdf) · [LaTeX](central-graphical-zonotopal-classification.tex) · [BibTeX](CITATION.bib) · [Verification](verification/README.md)

For every finite connected loopless multigraph over the real numbers, this preprint proves that its central graphical zonotopal algebra determines, and is determined by, its bridge-deleted graphical matroid. Algebra isomorphisms need not preserve the grading. The statement includes trees, bridges, parallel edges and disconnected bridge-free matroids.

The proof extracts cardinality-weighted cyclic-flat subspaces from nilpotence lengths and reconstructs the matroid by rank induction and marked SPQR gluing. It addresses Nenashev's graphical central classification conjecture (Conjecture 8), not the classification of arbitrary real vector configurations or internal zonotopal algebras.

Status: internally reviewed preprint; external mathematical review and formal peer review are pending. Nenashev's 2018 dissertation, Chapter 1 and especially Section 1.6, was inspected in full for the source-boundary revision. It states the relevant central inverse questions as conjectures and supplies no proof covering the graphical classification here. The dissertation does, however, already use nilpotence length in the external case; Section 1.1 distinguishes that precedent from this paper's central weighted cyclic-flat extraction and reconstruction. Finite verification checks support selected interfaces and are not substitutes for the general proof.

Topics: central zonotopal algebras, graphical matroids, cyclic flats, power ideals, SPQR decomposition, matroid reconstruction, Nenashev conjecture.
