# Positive Jacobi realizations for complete-bipartite symmetric edge polytopes

This preprint proves the canonical-line property for the Ehrhart polynomial of the type-A symmetric edge polytope of every complete bipartite graph $K_{m,n}$ with $\min(m,n)\leq5$. The new families are $K_{4,n}$ and $K_{5,n}$, obtained by explicit positive Jacobi path extensions; the smaller families are prior work.

For all integers $3\leq m\leq n$, it also proves positivity of the first two inverse tail coefficients. The second coefficient uses exact symbolic certificates. This does **not** establish the remaining tail coefficients or the complete-bipartite canonical-line conjecture.

The manuscript distinguishes the extra interlacing condition in Kölbl's 2021 and 2024 treatments from the unconditional families proved here. The cross-polytope recurrence and the complete-bipartite $h^*$ formula are used with attribution, not claimed as new.

Status: internally reviewed preprint; external mathematical review and formal peer review are pending. Code checks and finite regressions are not substitutes for the proof reductions in the manuscript.

Topics: Ehrhart polynomials, symmetric edge polytopes, complete bipartite graphs, canonical line, Jacobi matrices, interlacing, exact algebraic certificates.
