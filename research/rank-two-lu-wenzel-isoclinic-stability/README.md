## Result and boundary

For the complex Lu--Wenzel spectral problem, every rank-two matrix in arbitrary
dimension is reduced exactly to a core of size at most four. The new core
inequality is then proved on a coordered one-sided alignment layer and on the
complete isoclinic layer. For every fixed lower bound `epsilon > 0` on the
smaller singular value, the isoclinic result extends to a full-direction collar
of nearly equal principal angles. The manuscript also gives an explicit
two-component margin on the isoclinic layer.

The result does not cover the oppositely ordered alignment branch, does not give
a collar uniform as the smaller singular value tends to zero, and gives neither
an explicit nor an epsilon-independent collar threshold. It does not settle the
complete rank-two layer or the full Lu--Wenzel conjecture. The rank-one formula
used as a boundary calibration is prior work, and no worldwide-priority claim is
made.
